AI-Powered Upper Airway Ultrasound Assessment & Clinical Integration Solutions

Combining AI-powered imaging analysis with non-invasive ultrasound technology to enable faster and more informed obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) risk assessment.

Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) is a common yet frequently underrecognized sleep-related breathing disorder. While conventional sleep studies provide essential physiological data, upper airway ultrasound offers additional insights into airway anatomy and surrounding soft tissue structures, supporting a more comprehensive clinical evaluation.

MedAlliance provides AI-powered upper airway ultrasound assessment and clinical integration solutions that combine standardized ultrasound scanning, AI-assisted image analysis, and quantitative measurements. This approach enables healthcare professionals to obtain valuable upper airway anatomical information in approximately 10 minutes, supporting OSA risk assessment and subsequent clinical decision-making.

Through medical device deployment and clinical workflow integration, MedAlliance helps healthcare providers expand opportunities in sleep health screening, preventive care, and specialized clinical services.

Key Solution Advantages

10-Minute Rapid Assessment:
Obtain upper airway images while patients are awake, improving examination convenience and workflow efficiency.

AI-Powered Image Analysis:
Provide quantitative information on airway dimensions and surrounding soft tissue characteristics.

Non-Invasive Ultrasound Technology: Enable examinations without ionizing radiation or anesthesia.

Standardized Scan Positioning:
Support consistent image acquisition through assisted positioning technology.

Sleep Medicine & ENT Clinics

  • Q1. Can upper airway ultrasound help assess obstructive sleep apnea (OSA)?
    Yes. Upper airway ultrasound can provide anatomical and quantitative information about airway dimensions and surrounding soft tissues, supporting clinical OSA risk assessment. It serves as an additional evaluation tool alongside patient history, symptoms, and other clinical examinations.

  • Q2. How long does an AI-powered upper airway ultrasound assessment take?
    The assessment takes approximately 10 minutes. It is performed while the patient is awake and uses non-invasive ultrasound technology without ionizing radiation or anesthesia. Actual examination time may vary depending on the clinical workflow.

  • Q3. Can AI-powered upper airway ultrasound replace traditional sleep studies?
    No. Upper airway ultrasound provides complementary anatomical information and is not a standalone replacement for polysomnography (PSG) or other clinically indicated diagnostic sleep tests.

  • Q4.Which healthcare facilities can benefit from upper airway ultrasound assessment?
    Potential applications include sleep medicine clinics, ENT departments, health screening centers, preventive care facilities, and clinical research settings. The technology can support OSA risk evaluation and the planning of follow-up assessments.
