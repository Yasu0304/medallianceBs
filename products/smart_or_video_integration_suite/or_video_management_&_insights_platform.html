OR Video Management & Insights Platform

From scattered recordings to a unified, data-driven OR library.

Feature

  • Centralized OR case library
    Integrates case videos and related information from all operating rooms into a single management platform.
  • Multi-device access
    Allows medical staff to use phones, tablets, or computers to search and review cases anytime, anywhere.
  • PACS & DICOM integration
    Supports integration with PACS and DICOM so surgical videos can be easily used for reporting and teaching.
  • Smart replay & remote viewing
    Enables instant replay during surgery for emergency handling, and remote viewing to follow case progress.
  • Built-in cloud editing
    Provides an intuitive cloud editing function so physicians can quickly trim and prepare clips for education and presentations.
  • OR operations dashboard
    Displays real-time OR status and aggregates information onto a data board for multi-angle oversight of OR performance.
Contact Us

The OR Video Management & Insights Platform consolidates surgical videos and case information from every operating room into one secure environment. Medical staff can log in from mobile devices or PCs to search, review, and download cases whenever needed. Integrated with PACS and DICOM, the platform simplifies report creation and teaching material preparation. Real-time OR status displays and data boards give managers a clear view of OR utilization, case volumes, and surgical time trends, helping them reduce idle time and enhance overall OR efficiency.

  • Hospital-wide management of OR case videos
  • Teaching hospitals building structured surgical video libraries
  • OR performance monitoring and efficiency improvement
  • Surgeons' personal case statistics and learning-curve tracking
Client access Web-based access from smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
