AI-Powered Image Analysis: Transforming Upper Airway Ultrasound into Quantitative Insights

The AI-Powered Upper Airway Ultrasound Assessment System integrates ultrasound image acquisition, AI-assisted analysis software, and standardized positioning technology to enable quantitative evaluation of airway dimensions, tongue morphology, and surrounding soft tissue structures.
Through a dedicated analysis interface, the system visualizes anatomical characteristics across different upper airway regions and provides measurements of airway depth, width, and tissue thickness, allowing healthcare professionals to assess upper airway anatomy using objective quantitative information.
Beyond conventional ultrasound image visualization, the system combines region-of-interest analysis, quantitative measurements, and reporting capabilities to support upper airway anatomical assessment and obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) risk evaluation.

Multi-Region Upper Airway Analysis for Detailed Anatomical Assessment

The system supports ultrasound visualization and analysis of multiple upper airway regions, including the velum, oropharynx, tongue base, and epiglottis.
Through AI-assisted image analysis and region-of-interest (ROI) selection, healthcare professionals can quantitatively evaluate airway dimensions and surrounding soft tissue characteristics.

  • Airspace Depth:
    Measures the depth of the upper airway space.
  • Airspace Width:
    Evaluates the width of the upper airway space.
  • Tissue Thickness:
    Measures the thickness of the tongue and surrounding soft tissues.
  • Tissue Width:
    Provides quantitative measurements of soft tissue dimensions.
  • ROI Analysis:
    Enables image analysis and quantitative measurements within selected airway and tissue regions.

    • Laser-Assisted Positioning for Standardized Ultrasound Scanning

    The system incorporates a Laser Alignment Tool (LAT) featuring three laser alignment beams and an adjustable positioning mechanism to assist operators in aligning the patient's head and ultrasound probe.
    By supporting standardized scanning positions, the technology helps minimize image variations caused by positioning differences, improving consistency and comparability across examinations.

    Dedicated Analysis Interface with Quantitative Measurements and Reporting

    The system features a dedicated upper airway ultrasound analysis interface that displays airway and tissue regions of interest, corresponding measurement results, and relevant statistical information.
    Application-specific reports consolidate ultrasound images and quantitative measurements, enabling healthcare professionals to review upper airway anatomical information for clinical evaluation, further examination planning, and follow-up assessment.
