Visualizing Breath, Safeguarding Health

Provides continuous respiratory sound monitoring, transforming breathing sounds into visual spectrograms. Enhanced by AI deep learning for abnormal sound detection and real-time alerts, improving patient safety.

Disposable Sensors

2-year shelf-life sensor with dual electrical safety protection against shock and current leakage.

Small size with thin profile and skin-friendly adhesive ideal for patients with smaller necks or sensitive skin.
