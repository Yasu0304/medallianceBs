Contactless Fall Management & Health Insights

AI-powered fall solutions and bedside monitoring

Feature

  • No Cameras, No Wearables
    24/7 passive mmWave radar sensing for true privacy and comfort, even in low-light or bathroom settings.
  • Fall Detection & Prevention
    Real-time alerts, leave-bed and pre-fall warnings enable early staff response.
  • Bedside Health Insights
    Continuous non-contact tracking of respiration, pulse, sleep, agitation, and pressure injury risk scores and bed falls.
  • Workflow Integration
    Seamlessly connects with EHR/EMR and nurse-call systems, plus mobile apps and web dashboards.
  • Reimbursement-Ready Reports
    Generate RPM/RTM-oriented summaries to support quality programs and insurance claims (region-dependent).
  • Secure & Maintainable
    Data encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control, and cloud OTA (over-the-air) updates.
Download Contact Us

The Contactless Fall Management & Bedside Monitoring System uses advanced radar technology to deliver non-wearable, camera-free safety and health analytics for long-term care, smart wards, and home telehealth.

By continuously learning each resident's movement patterns, the system issues instant alerts when unassisted bed exits, nighttime activity, or irregular behaviors are detected—allowing caregivers to intervene before incidents occur.

Beyond fall detection, the platform generates behavioral and physiological insights such as respiratory and pulse trends, sleep quality, and pressure-injury risk scores, all visualized through EHR-integrated dashboards and mobile apps.

(*This product is classified as a Clinical Decision Support (CDS) system, not a diagnostic device. Reimbursement availability and features may vary by market or payer.)

  • Smart Wards / Post-Operative Care - Prioritize rounds, reduce alarm fatigue, improve patient mobility outcomes, and reduce readmissions
  • Senior Living & Long-Term Care - Enhance overnight safety, detect unassisted movements, and provide transparent incident records for families.
  • Telehealth / Home Monitoring (RPM/RTM) - Capture non-contact vital trends and deliver clinician-ready reports.
  • Bone & Pressure-Injury Prevention Programs - Integrate with AI imaging and wound-care protocols for proactive risk management.
