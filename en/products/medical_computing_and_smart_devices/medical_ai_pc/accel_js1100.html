ACCEL-JS1100

NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin platform with 5“ front touch screen for Medical AI imaging application

Feature

  • NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin platform with 64GB LPDDR5 or 32GB LPDDR5
  • Powerful AI computing performance with up to 275 TOPS
  • Support Front Screen
  • Support DP 1.4 or HDMI 2.0 video output
  • Compact size suitable for space limited area
  • Support HDMI 2.0 and 12G SDI video input
  • Medical certified with CE/FCC/UL
  • Support optional PCIe [x8] x1 for possible customization
  • Support built in speaker x 2
Download Contact Us

ACCEL-JS1100 is an ultra-compact medical AI edge computer, powered by NVIDIA® Jetson Orin™ NX, designed to bring intelligent processing to embedded clinical applications. It delivers high-efficiency performance for AI inference at the point of care.

  • Powered by NVIDIA® Jetson Orin™ NX module (up to 275 TOPS AI performance)
  • Fanless, space-saving design ideal for integration in medical devices
  • Medical-grade safety compliant (EN 60601-1) for clinical use
  • Rich I/O: HDMI, LAN, USB 3.2, CAN bus, RS-232
  • Supports popular AI frameworks: CUDA, TensorRT, DeepStream
  • Optimized for low-power, high-performance inference workloads
  • Smart Diagnostic Devices: Enables AI-powered analysis in compact imaging and test devices
  • Edge AI in Patient Monitoring: Detect anomalies locally with low-latency performance
  • Mobile & Handheld Medical Devices: Embedded AI for portable medical tools
  • Clinical Automation: Supports robotics, motion control, and sensor fusion
  • Point-of-Care AI: Deploy intelligent decision support directly where care is delivered
