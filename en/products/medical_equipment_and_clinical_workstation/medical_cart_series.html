MedAlliance offers a comprehensive Medical Cart Series with medical computer integration options, covering applications such as mobile nursing, telemedicine, medical information access, device mobility, and smart healthcare. Through flexible integration of medical carts, medical-grade computers, display devices, mounting solutions, and peripheral modules, customers can complete product selection, solution planning, and deployment more efficiently based on clinical workflows, mounted devices, channel requirements, and project goals.

Mobile Nursing Cart

Designed for ward rounds, bedside care, and nursing documentation, the mobile nursing cart can be integrated with medical-grade computers, touch displays, keyboard trays, barcode scanner holders, drawers, and power modules. It helps improve clinical workflow efficiency for nursing teams and can also serve as a standard configuration for healthcare institutions and channel partners deploying mobile nursing applications.

Telemedicine Cart

Designed for telemedicine, virtual rounding, remote consultation, and long-term care environments, the telemedicine cart can integrate cameras, displays, tablet computers, medical-grade computers, audio devices, network equipment, and power modules. It creates a mobile telemedicine workstation that supports smart healthcare and remote care service deployment.

Medical Computer Cart

Designed for medical information system terminals, point-of-care workstations, and bedside data access, the medical computer cart can be equipped with medical-grade computers, touch panel PCs, displays, keyboards, mice, and peripheral devices. It supports mobile clinical operations across different healthcare environments and helps customers build complete medical computer application solutions.

Tablet and Laptop Cart

Designed for patient education, mobile registration, outpatient support, care documentation, and long-term care facility management, the tablet and laptop cart can be configured with tablet holders, laptop trays, anti-theft locks, charging modules, and cable management. It provides lightweight and flexible medical mobility applications.

Display and Medical Device Cart

Designed for medical displays, compact medical instruments, patient monitoring devices, imaging display systems, and clinical support devices, the display and medical device cart can be configured with mounting arms, shelves, handles, casters, and cable management based on device weight, size, and operating requirements. It helps medical device brands and system integrators improve product application flexibility.

Modular Medical Cart Platform

Designed for medical device brands, system integrators, and project-based customers, the modular medical cart platform can integrate medical computers, displays, tablets, cameras, power modules, storage modules, mounting solutions, and peripheral accessories according to different clinical scenarios and device specifications. It supports OEM / ODM / CM project collaboration and customized product configuration.
