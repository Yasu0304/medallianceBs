Clinical Chemistry Analyzer

Complex Diagnostics, Simple Operation.

Feature

Transform your laboratory capabilities with the world's first and only full-spectrum, spectrum-based point-of-care (POC) Clinical Chemistry Analyzer. Combining advanced UV-Vis spectrum technology with an innovative microfluidic lab-on-a-chip design, this portable system delivers professional-grade, quantitative diagnostic results with unmatched precision and efficiency. Supporting up to 16 simultaneous assays while requiring minimal sample volume (＜90μL), it ensures real-time, on-site monitoring for clinical diagnostics and specialized testing. With high correlation to industry-standard analyzers, it guarantees reliable performance across diverse medical environments, offering seamless operation, speed, and accuracy in one powerful solution.
