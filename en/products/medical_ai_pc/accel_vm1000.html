ACCEL-VM1000

Dual GPU card Medical AI Box PC with Intel 13/12th Gen Core i9 CPU

Feature

  • Intel® 13/12 Gen Core i9 Processor
  • Support 4 x DDR4 3200 288 pin Long-DIMM up to 128GB
  • Optional 7" Front Screen with P-Cap Touch
  • Support front dual USB 3.0
  • Support GPIO and Remote Power Switch design for embedded use
  • Support Dual Nvidia RTX 6000 Ada cards and additional capture card installation
  • Support 4 x PCIe Gen4 Slots for high end graphic card and capture card integration
  • Support Medical Grade 1500W PSU for Dual GPU Cardand 700W PSU for Single GPU card
Download Contact Us

ACCEL-VM1000 is a high-performance medical AI video processing system, designed for real-time multi-channel imaging and intelligent analytics in surgical and diagnostic environments. Built with NVIDIA RTX™ GPU, it enables ultra-fast video inference and 4K streaming.

  • Powered by Intel® 12th Gen Core™ CPU and NVIDIA RTX™ A2000 GPU
  • Supports 4-channel HDMI video input and up to 2-channel HDMI output
  • AI acceleration for real-time image recognition, segmentation, and enhancement
  • Medical-grade certified (EN 60601-1) for clinical deployment
  • High-speed I/O: USB 3.2, LAN, HDMI, CAN, and optional DICOM/PACS support
  • Compact and fan-cooled design optimized for OR, endoscopy, and mobile carts
  • Compatible with major AI SDKs: TensorRT, CUDA, DeepStream, OpenVINO™
  • AI Surgical Imaging: Real-time object detection, tracking, and overlay during surgery
  • Endoscopy Video Processing: AI-assisted lesion detection and automatic annotation
  • Multi-Channel Video Recorder: Seamless integration with PACS/DICOM workflow
  • Telehealth & Remote Surgery: Low-latency 4K video streaming with embedded inference
  • Diagnostic AI Workstation: GPU-powered platform for AI model deployment and training
