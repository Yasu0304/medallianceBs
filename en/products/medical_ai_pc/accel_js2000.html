ACCEL-JS2000

NVIDIA IGX platform for Medical AI imaging application

Feature

  • NVIDIA IGX Orin Industrial Module with 250 TOPS AI Performance
  • Support RTX 6000 Ada GPU card with up to 1700 TOPS AI Performance
  • ConnectX-7 High Speed Edge Networking 2 x 100Gbe
  • Dedicated MCU to reassure the functional safety
  • Built-in BMC controller to provide a standard-based interface to system management and recovery
  • System Power design with 700W Medical PSU
  • Excellent thermal management to maximize the performance and lower the noise
  • 4K SDI capture card integration (Optional)
  • Support optional 7" front screen
  • Long Life cycle Support
  • Medical Certification
Download Contact Us

ACCEL-JS2000 is a compact yet powerful medical-grade AI box PC designed for edge computing in clinical environments. With NVIDIA® Jetson AGX Orin™, it enables real-time inference for diagnostic imaging, patient monitoring, and medical robotics.

  • Powered by NVIDIA® Jetson AGX Orin™ with up to 250 TOPS AI performance
  • Fanless and compact design for easy integration into medical devices
  • Medical-grade safety certification (EN 60601-1 compliant)
  • Rich I/O interfaces: HDMI, LAN, USB, CAN bus, and more
  • Supports AI frameworks including TensorRT, CUDA, and DeepStream
  • Optimized for 24/7 operation in clinical and surgical settings
  • Medical Imaging AI: Real-time enhancement, segmentation, and classification
  • Patient Monitoring Systems: Edge analytics for vitals, ECG, and telemetry data
  • Surgical Assistance: Supports AI-based navigation and robotic coordination
  • Diagnostic Devices: Compact design for integration into ultrasound, endoscopy, etc.
  • Telemedicine & Edge Analytics: On-device inference reduces latency and improves responsiveness
  • Hospital AI Infrastructure: Deployable in nurse stations, ICUs, and ORs for intelligent data flow
