MD-116E

12” Fanless Intel Pentium Medical Tablet

Feature

  • Intel® ELKhart Lake Pentium N6415
  • Dual hot-swappable batteries for 24/7 operation
  • Dual smart card readers for multiple-care workflow
  • Standard USB 3.0 Type-A / Type-C (Data & External Display)

A lightweight 11.6” mobile medical tablet built for daily clinical use. Designed with hygiene, mobility, and real-time data access in mind, it enables healthcare professionals to work efficiently at the point of care.

  • 11.6” Full HD multi-touch display with durable antimicrobial housing
  • Powered by Intel® Elkhart Lake CPU for efficient performance
  • Hot-swappable dual battery design with extended runtime
  • EN 60601-1 medical safety certified for clinical environments
  • Optional features: barcode scanner, smart card reader, RFID, and camera
  • Supports Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth, GPS for seamless connectivity
  • IP65-rated for water and dust resistance
  • Compact, fanless, and easy to disinfect - ideal for handheld or cart-mounted use
  • Telemedicine: Real-time remote consultation with AI-assisted diagnostics
  • Mobile Nursing: Patient data access and documentation at the bedside
  • Emergency & Ambulance: Rugged design suitable for high-mobility environments
  • Field Healthcare: Outdoor medical missions with reliable connectivity and battery life
  • Clinical Rounds: Lightweight, disinfectable design for daily ward use
