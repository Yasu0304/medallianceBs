Venus-244

23.8” Dual Hot Swappable Battery Medical Cart Computer

Feature

  • Intel 11th gen Tigerlake, fan less implementation
  • Windows 11 and IGEL Compliant
  • Dual Hot Swappable Batteries
  • Easy cleanable and anti-bacterial
  • Internal Imprivata compliant RFID reader (optional)
  • WiFi 6 (optional)
  • Compliant with all Venus batteries
  • Remotely Manageable by central iT departments
Download Contact Us

Venus-244 is a high-performance 24” medical cart computer designed to streamline clinical workflows at the point of care. Built with a fanless design, hot-swappable batteries, and medical-grade certification, it's ideal for mobile and hygienic environments.

  • 24” Full HD display with projected capacitive touch
  • Powered by Intel® 12th Gen Core™ i7/i5/i3 (Alder Lake-U) processors
  • Hot-swappable dual battery system for 24/7 operation
  • Medical-grade design with EN 60601-1, CE, and FCC Class B certifications
  • Fanless cooling system and IP65-rated front bezel for infection control
  • Rich I/O: USB 3.2, COM, HDMI, LAN, and optional expansion ports
  • Optional camera, RFID, smart card reader, and battery monitoring system
  • Supports wall, VESA, or medical cart mounting
  • EMR Access at Point of Care: Large touchscreen interface for fast data entry and visualization
  • Mobile Workstations: Long runtime with hot-swappable batteries, ideal for shift rotations
  • Telemedicine Carts: Optional camera and microphone for virtual consultations
  • Medication Dispensing: Secure ID verification via smart card or RFID reader
  • Operating Rooms / ICU: Fanless, easy-to-clean design meets infection control standards
  • Nursing Stations: Enables mobility without sacrificing performance or connectivity
