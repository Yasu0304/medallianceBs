AI Real-Time Breathing Monitoring System

The AI Real-Time Breathing Monitoring System revolutionizes patient care as the advanced AI-driven continuous respiratory tracker. This innovative technology provides healthcare professionals with real-time, intelligent monitoring, enhancing patient safety and setting a new standard in proactive respiratory care
