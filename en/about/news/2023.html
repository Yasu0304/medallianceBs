MedAlliance Partners with Intel to Showcase Smart Healthcare Ecosystem at 2023 Healthcare+ Expo Taiwan

MedAlliance was once again invited to participate in the Healthcare+ Expo Taiwan, this time as a featured ecosystem partner of Intel. Together with global leaders in technology and healthcare, MedAlliance showcased real-world applications of smart healthcare innovations, highlighting its role in building a scalable and intelligent care ecosystem.

Industry Co-Creation: Accelerating Smart Healthcare Transformation

Intel's exhibition focused on four key themes: Life Sciences, Medical AI, Smart Hospitals, and Cloud Security. Alongside 24 ecosystem partners, including MedAlliance, the showcase featured cutting-edge solutions powered by AI chipsets, OpenVINO™, and OpenFL. Leveraging its clinical-driven system integration expertise, MedAlliance demonstrated how AI technology, advanced medical devices, and real workflow applications can be combined to accelerate hospital digital transformation.

Embodying MedAlliance's Core Value: From Products to Solutions, From Technology to Consultancy

As an integrated healthcare solution consultant, MedAlliance not only delivers high-quality medical devices, but also supports healthcare providers in implementing intelligent care workflows. This joint exhibition marks a concrete step in MedAlliance's continued participation in global healthcare ecosystems—showcasing its strengths in product evaluation, system integration, on-site deployment, and long-term advisory services.

Global Outreach and Cross-Border Collaboration

Throughout the expo, Intel hosted more than 80 international matchmaking sessions, bringing together nearly 300 medical professionals and delegates from Southeast Asia, Europe, and the Americas. MedAlliance seized this opportunity to deepen connections with global partners, reinforcing its dual-focus strategy: export-driven growth and smart healthcare integration./p>
