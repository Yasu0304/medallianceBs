Medical Protective Mask Solutions

From routine clinical protection to high-risk healthcare environments, MedAlliance provides medical protective mask solutions planned around real application needs.

MedAlliance provides medical protective mask solutions built around real clinical needs, integrating Level 1 Medical Masks, Level 2 Medical Masks, Tie-on Surgical Masks, and Advanced Protection Medical Masks to support healthcare institutions, procurement teams, and channel partners in establishing a more complete and efficient protection strategy based on different risk levels, workflows, and care environments. Rather than simply supplying individual mask products, we focus on application-based selection guidance, protection configuration planning, and implementation support to make medical protection more aligned with real clinical practice.

Application-Based Protection Planning

Outpatient Care and Basic Protection

Outpatient Care and Basic Protection

Level 1 Medical Masks are designed for general healthcare environments and basic protection needs, with an emphasis on lightweight breathability and long-wear comfort. They are well suited as a foundational protection option for outpatient clinics and healthcare support areas.

Ward Care and Routine Clinical Workflows

Ward Care and Routine Clinical Workflows

Level 2 Medical Masks serve as an advanced protection option for environments with higher droplet exposure risk and more frequent patient care activities. They offer a balanced combination of protective performance and comfort, making them suitable for wards, nursing stations, and examination rooms.

Surgical and Procedural Environments

Surgical and Procedural Environments

Tie-on Surgical Masks provide a more secure fit and stable wearing performance, making them ideal for operating rooms, treatment areas, and procedural settings where long-duration use and reliable positioning are required.

High-Risk Healthcare Environments

High-Risk Healthcare Environments

Advanced Protection Medical Masks emphasize facial fit, filtration efficiency, and extended-wear comfort, making them an essential protective option for healthcare settings with elevated exposure risk.
